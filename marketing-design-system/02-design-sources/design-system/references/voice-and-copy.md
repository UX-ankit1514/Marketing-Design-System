# Voice and copy (from page "Writing and Grammar", v1.0.0)

"Your go-to rulebook for writing with clarity, professionalism, and a touch of warmth."

## Tone of voice — six traits
**Simple** (no jargon, no fluff) · **Direct** (say what matters, clearly) · **Friendly** (warm, approachable, human) ·
**Grateful & Respectful** (we value our clients and show it) · **Supportive** (we're here to make things easier) ·
**Reliable** (trustworthy, professional, calm).

## Do / Don't (verbatim pairs)
- ✅ "Let's get you started." ❌ "Initiate onboarding process."
- ✅ "Get free consultation." ❌ "Free consultation."  → CTAs are verb-first.
- ✅ "Welcome! We're glad you're here. Let's get your setup started—just a few easy steps." ❌ "Your account has been created. Please proceed to complete required formalities."
- ✅ "Oops, something went wrong. Let's fix it together." ❌ "An error occurred. Please try again later."
- ✅ "Something went wrong — but no worries, we're on it. Try refreshing or reach out to us."

## Casing
| Case | Rule | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Title Case | first letter of each major word | headlines, titles, **CTAs** ("Book a Free Consultation") |
| Sentence case | first word only | **body text** |
| UPPERCASE | sparingly, max impact | eyebrows/overlines, tiny labels |
| lowercase | informal, friendly | rare, tags/handles |
| kebab-case | sparingly | slugs, URLs |

## Punctuation
- Titles, sub-titles, headings: **avoid commas and full stops.**
- Ampersand: **no comma before "&"** → ✅ "Accounting & Tax" (or "accounting & tax") ❌ "accounting,& tax."
- Use an em dash with spaces for asides in microcopy ("… — but no worries, we're on it").

## Applying it to UI
- Buttons: verb-first, 1–4 words, Title Case, no full stop ("Get Started Today", "View Packages", "Book a Free Consultation", "Go to Dashboard").
- Errors: say what happened + what to do + reassure. Never blame the user. Inline field errors ≤ 12 words ("Enter a valid email address").
- Empty states: friendly one-liner + one primary action.
- Success: short, warm, specific ("Done! Your document is on its way.").
- Numbers & money: currency code + number ("AED 199 onwards"); dates unambiguous ("12 Oct 2026").
- Region: Arnifi operates in UAE/KSA/Oman/Qatar/USA/Singapore/Bahrain etc.; British spelling appears on the site ("customisation") — keep British.

## Known inconsistency
The written rule says headlines are Title Case, but the live landing headlines are **sentence case with an accent word**
("Our add-on services", "Why choose Arnifi"). Default to the **written rule** for new product UI; mirror the landing style
only when building marketing pages "like the website" — and say which you chose.
