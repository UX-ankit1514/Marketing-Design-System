# Template inventory

Source file: Figma `s2yQZ85TPB02pKuvfwTCgk` (Page 1). 58 candidate frames were inspected; **20 were selected** and rebuilt as editable templates, 38 were not (reasons below). Nothing was invented: every template links back to its Figma frame.

## How templates were chosen

The PRD weights were applied as guidance, not as a numeric model: variety of layout (25%), reusability for different content (25%), visual quality (20%), editable layer structure (15%) and coverage of marketing use cases (15%). Where several frames were the same design in a different colour or artwork, one was kept. Where one campaign existed in three sizes (story, square, link post), at most two sizes were kept so all three ratios are represented.
